Travefy is an itinerary tool used by travel advisors who present trips and let clients pay suppliers directly. WeTravel is a true all-in-one platform built for all multi-day tour operators, travel organizers, DMCs, and advisors – covering the full trip journey from itineraries to bookings, payments, supplier payouts, and more.
